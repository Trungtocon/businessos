# SME Offer Stack V4.5 (Lean V1)

**Date:** 2026-02-27

This document defines the "Dịch vụ marketing chuẩn hóa" offer cards to be displayed in the system (e.g., `/pricing` or Marketplace UI), mapping the AI Sprint Packs to a sellable service.

## 1. Starter Pack (Go-To-Market Setup)
**Target:** New SMEs needing foundational structure.
**Deliverable (Lead/CRM Pack):**
- 1 target ICP + Persona profile
- Funnel strategy (Top/Mid/Bottom)
- 2 automated email/Zalo sequences
- 7-day Sales follow-up checklist
**SLA:** Instant AI Draft + 24h Human Review.
**Price Placeholder:** $99 / 2.500.000 VND

## 2. Growth Pack (Content Engine)
**Target:** SMEs needing consistent inbound traffic.
**Deliverable (Content Pack):**
- 30-day Content Calendar
- 10 reusable Hooks & CTAs
- 10 multi-channel Post Drafts
- 3 short-form Video Scripts (TikTok/Reels)
**SLA:** Instant AI Draft + 48h Human Review.
**Price Placeholder:** $299 / 7.500.000 VND

## 3. Executive Pack (Growth Leadership)
**Target:** CEOs needing weekly operational clarity.
**Deliverable (Weekly Growth Report):**
- Executive dashboard KPI summary
- 3 strategic pivot/scale decisions
- 5 prioritized tasks for the marketing team
**SLA:** Instant delivery every Friday 5 PM.
**Price Placeholder:** $149/mo / 3.500.000 VND/mo
